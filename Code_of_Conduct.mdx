# Nightmare Nights North Code of Conduct

General Rules

- Nightmare Nights North is an event organized for charity and fandom artists.

- All attendees are required to follow:

- Hotel policies

- Local, state, and federal laws

- Any behavior that disrupts the event or damages relationships with venues, guests, or the public is strictly prohibited and may result in removal or banning from the event.

## Age Restriction

- Nightmare Nights North is strictly an 18+ event.

- All attendees, including badge holders, guests, vendors, artists, staff, and volunteers, must be at least 18 years of age on the date of the event.

- Valid government-issued photo identification (such as a driver’s license, passport, or state ID) proving age 18+ will be required at badge pickup/check-in.

- Minors (anyone under 18) are not permitted to attend under any circumstances, including as family members, accompanied guests, or for any other reason. There are no exceptions for parental consent, guardianship, or special circumstances.

## Behavior & Conduct

- Harassment of any kind is not tolerated. This includes:

- Non-consensual physical contact

- Stalking

- Verbal or written threats

- Intimidation or disruptive conduct

- Excessive profanity or use of slurs in public areas

- Actions that may endanger yourself or others are prohibited.

- All attendees must comply with instructions given by event staff or venue personnel. Clothing & Public Decency

- Attendees must wear appropriate clothing in public spaces:

- Footwear is required at all times

- Clothing must not expose, or appear to expose:

- Genitalia

- Buttocks

- Breasts

- Clothing and accessories must not display:

- Explicit sexual acts

- Genitalia

- Sexual fluids

- Extreme gore or unsanitary imagery

## Adult Content

- Adult or explicit material may not be displayed in public areas. (See Age Restriction section for full details on the 18+ policy.)

Weapons & Prohibited Items

- The following items are not permitted:

- Functional firearms

- Realistic replica firearms

- Projectile weapons such as bows, crossbows, or slingshots Photography & Recording

- Commercial photography or recording requires prior written permission from event organizers. Liability & Responsibility

- Nightmare Nights North is not responsible for:

- Lost, stolen, or damaged personal property

- Injuries sustained during the event

- Activities occurring within private hotel rooms, which remain the responsibility of the room holder

- All transactions between vendors and attendees are solely between those parties. Event Changes

- Panels, programming, and scheduled events may change without notice.

- Updates will be communicated through official channels such as email, the event website, or social media whenever possible.

## Rule Changes

- This Code of Conduct may be updated at any time:

- Prior to the event, attendees will be notified via email

- During the event, updates will be communicated as widely as possible

## Enforcement

- Violations of these rules, including the Age Restriction policy, may result in:

- Revocation of event credentials

- Removal from the event

- Involvement of venue security or law enforcement

- Permanent bans from future events

## Compliance

- Attendees must comply with any directive from staff to leave an area or cease an activity.

## Terms of Sale

- All badge holders must adhere to this Code of Conduct.

- Event badges remain the property of Nightmare Nights North until the conclusion of the event.

- All sales are considered final, except in rare or exceptional circumstances as determined by organizers.

- Badge transfers may be permitted through official channels.

- Attendees are responsible for monitoring their email and official communications for updates.

## Press Policy

- Members of the press must follow all rules outlined in this Code of Conduct.

- Press credentials are granted at the discretion of event organizers.

- Recording panels require the consent of all panelists.

- Recording in public convention areas is generally allowed, provided it does not violate attendee privacy or event rules.

## Staff Policy

- Staff members are volunteers and are expected to follow all event rules.

- Staff must behave professionally and represent Nightmare Nights North appropriately.

- Use of alcohol or other substances while on duty is prohibited.

- Staff who violate rules may face disciplinary action, including removal from their role or the event.

## Vendor Policy

- Vendors must be at least 18 years old, possess valid identification proving age, and comply with all applicable laws and event rules.

- Adult material must be concealed and not accessible to minors.

- Sale of counterfeit or unauthorized goods is prohibited.

- The sale of ready-to-eat food is not allowed due to venue restrictions.

- Vendors are responsible for their own taxes, licensing, and legal compliance.

- Violations may result in removal from the event without refund.
